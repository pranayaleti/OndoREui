# Self-Manage vs Ondo RE Calculator

> Compare annual net income from self-managing your Utah rental vs handing it to Ondo — including the hidden cost of your own time.

## Formula

Compare (a) self-manage: gross rent − direct expenses − opportunity cost of your time × hours/month against (b) Ondo: gross rent − Ondo fees − direct expenses.

## Inputs

- Monthly rent
- Direct operating expenses
- Hours/month you spend managing
- Value of your time
- Ondo management + leasing fees
