# Buying Power Calculator

> See how rate, down payment, and debts change your buying power.

## Formula

Max loan = target monthly payment × [(1 − (1 + r)^−n) / r]. Add your down payment for the maximum purchase price.

## Inputs

- Target monthly payment
- Interest rate (%)
- Loan term (years)
- Down payment
